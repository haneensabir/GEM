Halls of the Grand Egyptian Museum

The Grand Egyptian Museum is one of the largest archaeological museums in the world. It contains several exhibition halls that display thousands of artifacts from ancient Egyptian history, including the complete collection of King Tutankhamun.

Main Galleries

The museum contains 12 main galleries covering different historical periods. These halls focus on kingship, daily life, and religious beliefs in ancient Egypt

Tutankhamun Hall

Displays the treasures of King Tutankhamun including his golden mask.

The Grand Staircase

A unique space displaying large statues and monumental artifacts across multiple levels, offering an impressive visual experience.

Khufu Boat Museum

A special building showcasing the solar boats of King Khufu, moved from the Giza pyramids and displayed using modern technology.

Grand Hall – Ramses II

The Grand Hall is the main entrance of the museum. It features a massive statue of King Ramses II, welcoming visitors into the museum.

Children’s Museum

A special area designed for children to learn about ancient Egyptian history in a fun and interactive way.

Interactive Exhibition Hall

This hall uses modern technology to provide an immersive experience, allowing visitors to explore ancient Egypt through digital displays and interactive tools.
